## Executive investment read

**Salesforce (CRM) and HubSpot (HUBS) are the best of this four-company set for a CRM-focused relative valuation screen.** SAP provides adjacent, more diversified enterprise software context. Microsoft has an extensive cloud/infrastructure, productivity and consumer mix and is a **strategic platform benchmark, not a clean CRM valuation peer**. Their trading multiples must not be indiscriminately averaged into a valuation of an unfinished private CRM.

The two close CRM peer EV/LTM Sales observations are **4.94x** Salesforce and **3.06x** HubSpot, giving a purely descriptive **two-observation median = 4.00x**. This is *not* a selected valuation multiple for IMPERIAL Core: no independently verified CRM-SOFFT recurring revenue, revenue retention, churn, margin, enterprise value bridge, share count or comparable production business exists yet. Applying peer multiples to an imagined ARR would create a misleading company value.

## Where valuation premium appears supported — and where not

- **CRM (~4.94x)**: established large enterprise CRM, subscription/cRPO visibility and materially higher GAAP operating margin than HubSpot help justify a premium, but the acquired Informatica revenue contribution, ongoing capital return and competitive AI spending complicate a clean organic-growth comparison. Rerating hinges on actual organic billings, retention and monetizable Agentforce outcomes. A slowdown in core enterprise seat expansion or a deterioration in cash conversion would weaken the case.
- **HUBS (~3.06x)**: revenue growth is higher, but profitability is still much lower on a GAAP basis; the lower current multiple by itself does not establish undervaluation. Pricing/credit adoption, retention, net new customers, and the next Q3 print are key proof points. Weak seat expansion or a decline in net retention could impair the growth case.
- **SAP (~6.09x)**: current backlog and cloud ERP growth provide a defensible enterprise-quality signal, but its broad ERP mix is different from SMB-to-midmarket CRM; it should not anchor a small CRM valuation.
- **MSFT (~11.07x)**: hyperscale infrastructure, cloud margin, M365 and enterprise distribution dominate the multiple; **not** a CRM comparable. Azure acceleration is supportive, but AI-capex/FCF economics and cloud growth sustainability are the key downside sensitivities.

**Posture:** screening-only; no intrinsic fair-value estimate, target price, long/short recommendation or allocation decision. A public security price already reflects market expectations; a 4x revenue multiple does not imply CRM-SOFFT is worth four times imagined revenue.

## Strategic translation into CRM-SOFFT

**P0 — Enterprise trust (required before clients):** actual tenant/workspace identity, strict RLS and safe GRANT review, per-action AI Passport, Guardian Core, Approval Gateway, owner override/kill control, and privacy-safe Audit Ledger/ProofGraph. Keep commercial and CRYPTO IMPERIAL Ecosystem boundaries separate. A Google/GitHub profile, logo, agent avatar or claim of AI authorship is *never* an authorization credential.

**P1 — Unified Case-to-Cash:** one customer source receipt → verified request → qualified lead → case → domain federated orchestrator → scoped professional NCA team → independently accepted work → invoice/provided settlement evidence → `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED`. Do not book revenue from source counts, demo quotes, simulation passes, or “AI completed” statuses. Track cancellations and chargebacks.

**P2 — Agent economics:** implement `AgentUsageLedger` with cost per verified action, tokens/compute/time, independent QA yield, outcome-unit and client-accepted completion. Evaluate at least three price models (per seat, metered action and accepted outcome), but **do not enable billing** without explicit Owner approval, payment-provider reconciliation and a lawful merchant arrangement. Provide explainable cost caps and a customer-facing spending shield.

**P3 — Multi-vertical reusable product:** Capital Core real estate (licensed human review, transaction/advertising compliance), AI services, CRM automation, brokerage, and digital-product teams share the same core ledger and different skill/policy packs. VELION and CONSTRUCTOR AI can provide generation but must pass isolation, verification and release approval. NCA avatars and domains can differ; use **the GitHub-approved single canonical IMPERIAL Core emblem** without redrawing it.

**P4 — Measurable customer value:** track lead qualification precision, stage-to-stage conversion, median time-to-first-response, independent-QA pass/fail, human rework, provider-backed confirmed payments, per-outcome gross margin, retained customers and action/cost budget. No percentage or price claim without production evidence.
